OUR FARM

Rooted in Nature. Crafted with Royalty

Growpure Farm is a thoughtfully curated agro-tourism retreat that blends royal living
with authentic rural experiences, surrounded by lush farms, forests, and open countryside.

OUR PHILOSOPHY

What Makes Us Unique

01

Working Farms with Interactive Activities

Walk through living farmlands, harvest seasonal vegetables, and experience the rhythm of rural life hands-on.

02

Royal Farm Stays

Stay in antique-inspired cottages draped in lush creepers, blending heritage architecture with nature's canopy.

03

Private Safari & Forest Experiences

Venture deep into untouched forest trails on guided safaris, discovering wildlife and ancient trees in their natural habitat.

04

Authentic Desi Cuisine from Our Farm

Savour meals prepared with freshly picked produce — from traditional Rajasthani thalis to farm-to-table dining under the stars.

05

Peaceful Pondside & Riverside Experiences

Unwind by serene ponds and flowing streams, where the only sounds are rustling leaves and birdsong at dawn.
