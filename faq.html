Frequently Asked Questions

How can I book a reservation?

For reservation-related queries, you can call us at +91 99503 83019 or email us at bookgrowpureluxuryfarm@gmail.com.

Where is the property located?

Our resort farm stay is located in Kushalgarh, Banswara, surrounded by natural beauty and peaceful surroundings.

What activities are available at the property?

We offer a wide range of activities, including animal petting, cattle and agro farming, trekking & hiking, jungle safari, farm activities, archery, adventure sports, meditation, and spiritual experiences.

Is the property suitable for families and children?

Yes, our farm stay is family-friendly and offers safe and engaging activities for guests of all ages.

What kind of accommodation is available?

We offer luxury antique interior–based cottages within the farm, featuring open balconies, providing a perfect blend of comfort, elegance, and nature.

Are meals provided during the stay?

Yes, all four meals are available, including breakfast, lunch, snacks, and dinner.
